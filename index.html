SilentVoice

SilentVoice is a lip-reading app that helps patients who can't produce voice communicate with the world.

The problem

The problem is that some patients today cannot produce voice and therefore cannot communicate with medical staff and the rest of the world.

The product

It works by reading the patient's lips and then reconstructing its voice.
